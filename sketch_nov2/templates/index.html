ASTRA
Autonomous Spaceflight Telemetry & Risk Assessment

A comprehensive multi-tiered medical intelligence system engineered for deep-space astronaut health monitoring, clinical decision support, and Ground Mission Control oversight.

❤ Real-Time Biosensors

Integrated nRF52840, MAX30102 PPG, and BMP390 altimeter streaming cardiopulmonary metrics and cabin acoustics.

☑ 12 Health Indicators

Clinical reference models across cardiovascular, fluid, glucose, radiation, DEXA bone density, and circadian indicators.

⚗ Cognitive Readiness

Standardized neuro-cognitive vigilance tasks: reaction time, tracking with distractors, and sequence memory.

🌎 Earth Connection

Sensory pacing relaxation environment with 4-4-6 breathing guidance and generative ambient acoustic therapy.

📡 Interplanetary Messaging

Mission-accurate store-and-forward communications accounting for speed-of-light signal delays between Moon/Mars and Earth.

🚑 Clinical Decision Engine

51 medical scenario testbeds, prescription adherence logging, and autonomous flight surgeon advisory protocols.

ASTRA is an academic prototype engineered for the NASA Space Apps Challenge. Non-clinical simulation system.
